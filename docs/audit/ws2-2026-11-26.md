# Workstream 2 re-audit, 26 Nov 2026

A fresh audit of the Dashboard, the command bar as primary navigation, saved
views and unified search, against the overhaul brief, docs/BENCHMARK.md and
the standing rules. It follows the 16 Nov build (docs/audit/dashboard.md and
docs/audit/command-bar.md). Sources: a code read (every finding checked in
the code) and the running app at 1440 px and 390 px. Status is updated as
items ship.

## Solid (no change needed)

- **Overview panels:** market state, RBA and macro, holdings and watchlist
  all carry badges, and show "—" for missing values.
- **Movers:** sorted by size of move, with the cut-off counted.
- **Brief summary:** read from cache, with time and zone.
- **Coming up:** times carry their zone, and the badge is dated.
- **Data health:** a status line.
- **Arrange:** order kept and reset.
- **Overview keys:** don't fire while typing.
- **Since-last-visit empty states:** first visit and nothing changed are
  handled.
- **Command bar:** combobox semantics and a preview of what Enter opens.
- **Unified search:** covers the glossary, views, events and Relationships.
- **Phone width:** no page scrolls sideways in any module (all 19 measured
  at 390 px).
